ABOUT THIS PILOT

Useful local work, with a clear source

The atlas brings together public volunteering routes relevant to preparedness, community support and recovery. It is a research pilot for discussion with the National Emergencies Trust; inclusion does not establish a NET partnership or endorsement.

What the listings tell you

A published opportunity is an activity advertised by its source. Enquire with host means dates, location or places need arranging. Ongoing commitment may involve training or regular attendance. Broker and platform entries help you find activities; they are not vacancies.

“Atlas assessment” explains our view of an activity’s community resilience relevance. Employee format, duration and requirements are taken from the linked source where stated. Unknown details remain “Confirm with host”.

How records stay current

Every record has a source-check date and a review deadline. Past-deadline, closed and overdue-for-review records are hidden from the default results automatically. Choose “Needs review / ended” to inspect them. A source check does not confirm live places with the host.

Location and coverage

The pilot covers Greater London, the Somerset Council area, and Cumberland plus Westmorland and Furness. A marker can represent a venue, an organisation’s office, or an approximate area. Postcode-derived positions are approximate; confirm the address and meeting point with the host. Office proximity is not service coverage; the host’s coverage note is shown in each record. Postcode data attribution.

No matching listings means no matching records in this pilot. It does not show an absence of volunteering or community capacity.
