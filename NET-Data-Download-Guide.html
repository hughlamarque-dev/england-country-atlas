ENGLAND ATLAS · DATA DEVELOPMENT

Additional data that would help most

The atlas now includes local-authority deprivation, Census disability and household car availability, plus community-foundation and resilience-forum directories. The next useful addition is verified flood exposure.

Source pages and the rivers-and-sea download options were checked on 18 September 2026. The flood products below have not yet been ingested or validated in the atlas.

1. Start with two summary downloads

These are the smallest, clearest next step for comparing local authorities. Keep every workbook tab, including the definitions and calculation notes.

Use the Download button under “Full downloads and supporting documentation”. Upload the completed ZIPs. Their geography, categories and denominators still need inspection before integration. These are long-term risk summaries, not counts of people affected by a current incident.

2. For the actual flood map, download the spatial layers

  1. Open the product page and choose “Download data by area of interest and format”.
  2. For rivers and sea, the selector currently offers “Full dataset”, a drawn polygon, or an uploaded shapefile. GeoPackage is an available output format and is the preferred choice for this project.
  3. For a manageable first spatial build, select one pilot area and use the same footprint for both flood products. Whole-England downloads can be much larger.
  4. Include the completed geospatial file, the product-description PDF and any accompanying metadata. Preserve the original filenames, coordinate system and class fields.

A receipt, preview image or .lyr styling file does not contain the flood geometries. The requested deliverable is the completed dataset download. Climate-change products should be supplied separately with their scenario metadata.

3. Data that only NET or its partners can confirm

A verified partner-coverage spreadsheet would be more useful than another list of office addresses. Suggested columns:

IdentityOrganisation name; organisational website; public enquiry contact
CoverageAreas served, preferably official area codes; any exclusions; geographical source
OfferSupport types; eligibility; referral route; opening hours; language and accessibility provision
VerificationWho confirmed the information; verification date; source link; permission to publish

NET-approved aggregate grant data could support a later recovery view: reporting period, geographical code, support type, applications, awards, amounts, payment timing and outstanding needs. Personal case records belong in a separate restricted system; the current atlas is public.

What you do not need to download again

The corrected IoD2025 authority summaries, Census TS038 and TS045, UKCF office directory and Cabinet Office English LRF directory are already included. Further Census topics and small-area deprivation can be retrieved from their public sources during the next data-development pass.

The atlas remains England-only. Census and deprivation values for Barnsley and Sheffield are withheld where the source codes do not match the atlas. Their values should be reconciled through authoritative geography documentation, not a name-only match.
